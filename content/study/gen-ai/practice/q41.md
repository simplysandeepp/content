## What's actually being asked
A compare-and-contrast under three specific lenses: training instability, image quality, and controllability — not a general StyleGAN essay.

## Answer

### Training instability

**Traditional GAN**: trained on the minimax objective
`min_G max_D V(D,G) = 𝔼_{x∼p_data}[log D(x)] + 𝔼_{z∼p_z}[log(1 − D(G(z)))]`.
Two classic failure modes show up here. First, if the generator uses the original saturating loss (minimizing `log(1 − D(G(z)))`), gradients vanish early in training when `D` easily rejects `G`'s poor samples — `G` barely learns. In practice this is fixed by the **non-saturating loss**: `G` instead maximizes `log D(G(z))`, which gives much stronger gradients exactly when `G` needs them most. Second, training the full deep generator/discriminator stack at full resolution from the start is fragile — small imbalances between `G` and `D` compound across many layers, leading to mode collapse or non-convergence.

**StyleGAN (and its Progressive-GAN backbone)**: addresses the second problem by growing the network progressively — starting at low resolution and adding layers as training stabilizes at each scale, rather than optimizing the whole deep stack at once. On top of that, AdaIN-based style injection keeps per-layer activation statistics normalized and well-behaved, which further smooths gradient flow compared to a generator that just concatenates a raw latent into feature maps.

### Image quality
Traditional GAN quality is capped by the single-shot architecture: one latent, pushed through one upsampling stack, has to carry every attribute at every scale simultaneously, and any instability during training directly shows up as artifacts. StyleGAN's quality improvements come from the same architectural changes that fix stability — progressive growth means each resolution is learned on a reasonably well-trained foundation rather than from scratch, style injection via normalization keeps feature statistics consistent layer-to-layer, and the dedicated per-pixel noise channel means the style pathway doesn't have to also represent stochastic micro-texture, freeing it to represent cleaner, more coherent structure.

### Control over generated content
This is where the gap is widest. A traditional GAN offers **no** structured control — the only lever is resampling a new `z`, which changes everything at once because `Z` is entangled (forced into a fixed Gaussian prior that doesn't match real attribute variation). StyleGAN introduces:
- a **mapping network** `f: Z → W`, giving a more disentangled space to edit in;
- **per-resolution style injection**, so coarse layers (pose, shape), middle layers (finer structure), and fine layers (colour, texture) can be controlled independently;
- **stochastic noise inputs**, isolating random micro-detail from semantic attributes;
- **style mixing**, letting two latents' coarse and fine styles be combined deliberately.

## Exam tip
When the question explicitly names three lenses (instability / quality / control), answer under exactly those three headings — don't merge them, even though the same architectural changes (progressive growth, style injection, noise) are the cause under all three. Examiners are checking you can trace one cause to multiple effects.
