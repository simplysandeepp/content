## What DevFest Is

DevFest is Google's annual global developer festival — thousands of GDG chapters run local versions simultaneously around the world. The structure is yours to design; the community is Google's. It's the year's biggest event for any GDG chapter.

For us at GDG On Campus GU, it was our most ambitious event of the year.

## The Work Behind It

Organizing an all-day developer conference as a student team is genuinely difficult. There's a coordination problem at every level: securing a venue, scheduling speakers, managing registrations, running the day-of logistics, and keeping everything on time.

**Speaker curation** — We wanted practitioners over theorists. People who could talk about what they're actually building, not about concepts from a slide deck.

**Workshop tracks** — Ran parallel tracks so attendees could choose depth (technical workshops) or breadth (talk sessions). Different audiences need different formats.

**Day-of execution** — The gap between a good plan and a good event is execution. We'd coordinated rehearsal runs for the AV setup, speaker timing, and transition management.

## The Day

A full room. Talks that ran to Q&A overflows because people were engaged. Workshop participants who didn't leave at the breaks. That's the signal that it worked.

The feedback that mattered most: several first-year students said it was the first time they'd felt like part of a tech community. That's the point.
