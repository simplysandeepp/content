## What Is a Cloud Study Jam?

Google runs Cloud Study Jams globally — structured, self-paced learning tracks through Google Cloud Skills Boost. Students earn skill badges by completing labs in real GCP environments. GDG chapters run the facilitation layer: coordinating registration, tracking progress, keeping participants accountable, and helping when they get stuck.

We ran ours across three weeks.

## How It Worked

**Week 1 — Orientation and setup** — Getting everyone into Google Cloud Skills Boost, claiming free access, picking a learning path. A lot of first-session hand-holding is just setup. We built the leaderboard during this week so progress became visible.

**Weeks 2–3 — Independent progress with open support** — Participants worked through labs at their own pace. We ran two drop-in Q&A sessions per week where anyone could show up, share their screen, and get unstuck.

**The Leaderboard** — Built a real-time leaderboard so participants could see where they ranked among peers. It made the whole thing feel like a shared effort rather than solo homework.

## What Made It Work

Making progress visible was the single biggest driver of completion rates. When someone sees their name move up the leaderboard after completing a lab, they come back for the next one. Gamification, but the stakes are actual skills and a Google badge.

The drop-in support sessions also mattered more than I expected. A few participants were one stuck moment away from giving up. Having someone to unblock them in 5 minutes kept them in.
