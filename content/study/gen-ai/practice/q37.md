## What's actually being asked
What is the one big architectural idea that makes StyleGAN different, and how exactly does it diverge from a vanilla GAN generator.

## Answer

### Traditional GAN generator — the baseline
In a traditional GAN, a single latent vector `z ~ p_z` (usually `N(0,I)`) is fed directly into the generator network, which is a stack of transposed convolutions that progressively upsamples it into an image. The value function is the usual
`min_G max_D V(D,G) = 𝔼_{x∼p_data}[log D(x)] + 𝔼_{z∼p_z}[log(1 − D(G(z)))]`.

Yaha problem yeh hai ki `z` has to simultaneously encode *every* factor of variation — pose, identity, lighting, background — while being squeezed into a fixed, simple Gaussian shape. Real-world factors of variation don't actually follow that shape, so `Z` ends up **entangled**: moving along one direction in `z` changes multiple unrelated attributes at once, and there's no mechanism to control one attribute independently.

### StyleGAN's key architectural idea
StyleGAN's core idea is to stop feeding the latent code directly into the generator, and instead **inject it repeatedly, as "style," at every resolution level of the synthesis network**, via normalization. Three pieces make this work:

1. **Mapping network `f: Z → W`** — 8 fully-connected layers transform `z` into an intermediate latent `w`. Because `w` isn't forced to match a fixed prior, the network can warp the space so it better matches the real, disentangled factors of variation in the data.
2. **Style modulation (AdaIN) at every layer** — the synthesis network doesn't start from `z`/`w` at all; it starts from a learned constant 4×4×512 tensor. At each resolution, `w` is passed through a learned affine transform `A` to produce a style `(y_s, y_b)`, which modulates normalized feature maps:
   `AdaIN(x_i, y) = y_{s,i} · (x_i − μ(x_i)) / σ(x_i) + y_{b,i}`.
3. **Explicit per-pixel Gaussian noise**, added at each layer independently of style, to control purely stochastic detail (exact hair-strand placement, freckles) without touching semantic attributes.

### Direct differences from a traditional GAN
- **Where the latent enters**: traditional GAN feeds `z` once, at the input; StyleGAN starts from a learned constant and injects style at *every* layer.
- **Latent space used for control**: traditional GAN edits are done in entangled `Z`; StyleGAN exposes a more disentangled intermediate space `W`.
- **Granularity of control**: traditional GAN gives none — one `z` controls everything at once; StyleGAN's per-resolution injection means different layers end up controlling different levels of detail (coarse vs. fine).
- **Stochastic detail**: traditional GAN has no separate channel for it; StyleGAN has explicit per-layer noise inputs.
- **Normalization-driven generation**: no generator layer in StyleGAN depends directly on `z` — only on `w` (via styles) and noise. This is the detail examiners like to probe.

## Exam tip
If asked to name "the" key idea in one line: *style-based injection at every resolution, through a learned, disentangled intermediate latent `w`, instead of a single latent fed once at the input.* Don't forget to mention that the synthesis network's actual input is a learned constant, not `z` — that's the detail that trips people up.
