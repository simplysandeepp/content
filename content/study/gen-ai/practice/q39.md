## What's actually being asked
List StyleGAN's improvements over a traditional GAN under four named headings: style-based control, progressive (multi-scale) control, image quality, and diversity.

## Answer

### Baseline recap
A traditional GAN feeds a single latent `z ~ p_z` straight into the generator, trained against
`min_G max_D V(D,G) = 𝔼_{x∼p_data}[log D(x)] + 𝔼_{z∼p_z}[log(1 − D(G(z)))]`.
One vector controls the whole image, entangled, with no per-attribute handles.

### 1. Style-based control
StyleGAN maps `z` through a mapping network `f: Z → W` (8 FC layers) into an intermediate latent `w`, which is then injected at every synthesis layer via learned affine transforms into AdaIN-style modulation:
`AdaIN(x_i, y) = y_{s,i} · (x_i − μ(x_i)) / σ(x_i) + y_{b,i}`.
Because `w` isn't tied to a fixed prior shape, `W` ends up more disentangled than `Z` — individual directions correspond more cleanly to individual semantic attributes, something a plain `z`-fed generator can't offer.

### 2. Progressive, multi-scale control
Style is injected independently at every resolution level of the synthesis network (4×4 up to 1024×1024). This naturally splits control by scale: coarse layers (4×4–8×8) govern pose and face/body shape, middle layers (16×16–32×32) govern finer structural features, and fine layers (64×64–1024×1024) govern colour and micro-texture. A traditional GAN has no equivalent — you can't selectively edit "just the hairstyle" or "just the lighting" because there's no per-scale injection point.

### 3. Image quality
Quality gains come from several compounding factors: normalization-based style injection keeps feature statistics well-behaved layer to layer (unlike raw concatenation of a latent into feature maps), the synthesis backbone grows resolution progressively (stabilizing training at each new scale rather than learning all resolutions at once), and a dedicated per-pixel noise channel absorbs stochastic detail so the style/`w` pathway doesn't have to "fight" to represent both identity and random micro-texture in the same signal. Qualitatively, this produces noticeably sharper, more coherent high-resolution output than a traditional GAN generator trained end-to-end on a single latent.

### 4. Diversity
Two explicit mechanisms give StyleGAN diversity control a traditional GAN lacks:
- **Stochastic noise inputs** at every layer generate independent random micro-variation (hair strand flow, freckle placement) across samples that otherwise share the same `w`.
- **Style mixing** during training (using two different `w` vectors split across a random layer crossover) prevents the network from assuming adjacent-layer styles are correlated, and at inference lets you deliberately mix two sources' coarse/fine attributes to get new combinations.
- The **truncation trick** — moving `w` towards the mean `w̄` — gives an explicit quality/diversity dial: truncate more for safer, more "average" high-quality samples, less for more diverse but occasionally weaker ones.

A traditional GAN gives you none of these dials; its only diversity source is whatever is implicit in sampling a fresh `z`.
